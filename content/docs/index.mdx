Welcome to **AEdocs** — a single, modern home for After Effects expressions and scripting documentation.

The source material comes from [docsforadobe](https://github.com/docsforadobe), who maintain two excellent but separate mkdocs sites: one for the [expression reference](https://github.com/docsforadobe/after-effects-expression-reference) and one for the [scripting guide](https://github.com/docsforadobe/after-effects-scripting-guide). Both are great references, but mkdocs' Material theme has always felt a bit dated, and bouncing between two separate sites to look things up got old. So this rebuilds both into Fumadocs — same content, restyled and merged into one searchable, unified docs tree.

Fumadocs also happens to support optional AI-friendly extras, like per-page `llms.txt`/markdown exports and "Open in ChatGPT / Claude" actions. They're opt-in, not the default reading experience — there if you want to feed a page to an LLM, easy to ignore otherwise.
